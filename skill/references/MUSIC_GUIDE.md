# Music / Sound Guide

The image supplies spectacle. Sound supplies **weight and punctuation**.

Read that as a division of labour, not a metaphor: **sound is the punctuation,
the image is the spectacle.** The frame is the sentence; the score is the comma,
the full stop, the dash. Punctuation that argues with its sentence is a defect,
which is why almost every failure mode below is a piece of music trying to be the
spectacle as well.

For this visual language, avoid default cyberpunk techno. It competes with the
frame instead of articulating it.

## Prefer: sound-design-first scoring

- sparse terminal pulses
- dry digital clicks
- low sub pressure
- metallic transients
- granular bursts
- hard stops
- sudden silence
- short acceleration ramps

Silence is a beat, not an absence. The strongest moment in a high-impact edit is
usually the frame *after* a hard stop, not the impact itself.

## Avoid

- four-on-the-floor rhythm under a dense frame
- synthwave pads
- orchestral trailer swells
- riser-into-drop clichés
- continuous texture with no gaps

All of these fill the space the images need. A dense picture plus dense music is
not intensity; it is noise.

## Sync to the chain, not to a bar grid

The transformation chain already contains the edit's structure. Each mechanism's
peak is a real peak — the implosion, the shockwave, the giant word arriving past
frame. Cut sound to those events rather than to a tempo.

The engine's density curve is the intended envelope:

```bash
ascii-h3 plan "…"          # prints beats, exit states and the gate
```

A major swing every 2–3 seconds is also the sound-design edit rhythm. The gate's
`contrast` check reports that envelope as a number
(`density spans 0.12–0.94 with 9 major swing(s)`) — place a sound event where the
curve bottoms out and where it peaks, and leave the transitions between them
comparatively bare.

## Compact Suno direction template

Copy, adjust, and keep it short:

```
Experimental electronic sound-design score, minimal and cinematic. Sparse digital
pulses, dry clicks, deep sub impacts, granular noise, metallic transients,
irregular rhythm, long empty spaces, sudden hard stops, rising density, then
abrupt collapse to silence. No vocals, no EDM, no synthwave, no orchestral
trailer. Cold, precise, intelligent, unstable.
```

## Mixing note

If the visuals are at maximum density, pull the music **down**, not up. Loud
dense music under a dense frame reads as noise; quiet music under a dense frame
reads as tension, because the audience can still hear the image.
